## E

### Eastern Watchtower
**In short:** The guard tower west of the Spider Gate, where you met Hai-er Ha.
**Type:** Place. A guard tower about a mile west of the [Spider Gate](#spider-gate). Two guards died there the night of the festival. [Hai-er Ha](#hai-er-ha) survived inside.

### Eight Practices
**In short:** Eight folk rules that keep minor spirits away during the season of ghosts.
**Type:** Custom. Folk rules Willowshorers follow during the [season of ghosts](#season-of-ghosts) to keep minor spirits away. [Hai-er Ha](#hai-er-ha) made you recite four to prove you weren't monsters.
1. Don't call a ghost a ghost. Greet it as a friend.
2. Don't pat people on the head or shoulders.
3. Stay out of water when a ghost is near.
4. Don't eat food with two sticks standing up in it.
5. Don't lean against walls during the day.
6. Don't whistle at night.
7. Don't leave laundry out at night.
8. If someone calls your name from behind you at night, don't turn around.

**Seen in:** [The road into town](history.md#the-road-into-town).

## R

### Reenactment Festival
**In short:** The end-of-spring festival that fake-kidnaps "abductees" to fool evil spirits. You were this year's abductees.
**Type:** Custom. Willowshore's end-of-spring tradition. Townsfolk in paper masks fake-kidnap a few chosen "abductees" and leave them in the woods overnight. The town stages a panic, then ransoms them with breakfast at dawn. It's meant to trick evil spirits into thinking the town has been haunted already. You were this year's abductees.
**Seen in:** [Willowshore basics](basics.md#the-reenactment-festival).

### Reputation
**In short:** Your standing with Northridge and Southbank. It sets the way townsfolk treat you.
**Type:** Rule. Both halves of town, [Northridge](#northridge) and [Southbank](#southbank), keep track of how you treat them. Helping their people raises your standing. Hurting them, or the things they care about, lowers it. Your standing sets how townsfolk greet you, how hard it is to win them over, and how well you earn money in town.

| Standing | Townsfolk start | Persuasion DCs | Earn Income |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revered | Helpful | −10 | +3 |
| Admired | Friendly | −5 | +2 |
| **Liked** | Friendly | −2 | +1 |
| Ignored | Indifferent | — | — |
| Disliked | Unfriendly | +2 | −1 |
| Hated | Unfriendly | +5 | −3 |
| Hunted | Hostile | +10 | Can't Earn Income |

Your current standing is on [Where we are now](now.md#your-standing-in-town).
