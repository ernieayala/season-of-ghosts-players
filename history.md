## Chapter 1: To Light the Night

> *The festival ran as it does each year, with paper masks and a mock kidnapping. You bedded down in the woods expecting breakfast at dawn. While you slept, the fog rolled in and monsters took the town.*

### The morning after the festival

- You woke in a forest clearing east of town, wrapped in last night's straw mats and red blindfolds, on the first day of summer. The clearing was not where you fell asleep.
- [Choe Chung-hu](compendium.md#choe-chung-hu), the miller who was to bring the dawn "ransom" of breakfast, never came.
- Two giant orange centipedes attacked. As they hissed, the hisses became words: the parts of your bodies they wanted to eat. You killed them.

### The road into town

- Fog rolled in as you reached the Duyue River bridge, and it thickened as you crossed. This was the [mirage mist](compendium.md#mirage-mist).
- Someone recalled an old nursery rhyme about [Lazy Adou](compendium.md#lazy-adou), who forgot to light the lamp and let the ghosts out to play.
- At the [Spider Gate](compendium.md#spider-gate), the stone guardian [Ugly Cute](compendium.md#ugly-cute) was gone. The lantern it held lay unlit in the grass. Deep tracks led down the road and into the river. You didn't follow them.
- At the [Eastern Watchtower](compendium.md#eastern-watchtower), two town guards lay dead, their legs swollen, beside two dead [jinkins](compendium.md#jinkin). The surviving guard, [Hai-er Ha](compendium.md#hai-er-ha), made you recite the [Eight Practices](compendium.md#eight-practices) to prove you weren't monsters.
- Hai-er asked you to check on her family. Her daughter was with [Granny Hu](compendium.md#hu-ban-niang-granny-hu), and her husband was at the [Matsuki estate](compendium.md#matsuki-estate). She gave you a ladder feather token, a shining crossbow bolt, and a cask of lantern oil.
- You left her at the tower and called her a coward.
- Over these days you saw all three signs of the dark Lantern: the daytime [mirage mist](compendium.md#mirage-mist), the [crimson moon](compendium.md#crimson-moon) at night, and [blood rain](compendium.md#blood-rain).

### The two elders

- At the [Matsuki estate](compendium.md#matsuki-estate) in [Southbank](compendium.md#southbank), [Old Matsuki](compendium.md#matsuki-shou-old-matsuki) briefed you.
  - He told you the story of Master [Zhi Hui](compendium.md#zhi-hui) and the [Eternal Lantern](compendium.md#eternal-lantern).
  - The Lantern needs three blessed copper coins to burn again. He gave you three coins and taught you the prayer to bless them.
  - He wanted [Northridge](compendium.md#northridge) contacted and [Ugly Cute](compendium.md#ugly-cute) found, with help from the Silvermist hunters.
  - About the monsters: the [buso](compendium.md#buso) on the bridge is a heavy drinker, so drug him. The jinkins fear him, so call him out alone.
- You crossed the river at the [dam](compendium.md#willowshore-dam) into Northridge.
- At the [Trade Office](compendium.md#trade-office), [Granny Hu](compendium.md#hu-ban-niang-granny-hu) marked monster sightings on a map.
  - She named [Mo Douqiu](compendium.md#mo-douqiu) as the monsters' "brain," and she scoffed at the idea of a statue waking up.
  - Her aide, the cleric [Kim Gu-won](compendium.md#kim-gu-won), told you that Calistria, Kofusachi, and Shelyn blessed the Lantern's original coins.
  - She asked you to check on [Doctor Dami](compendium.md#doctor-dami) and to find her missing grandchildren.
- You met both elders and sided with neither.

### Missions for the town

- **Granny Hu's grandchildren:** her three grandchildren and a friend had passed out in a lumber yard near the [Mushroom House](compendium.md#mushroom-house). Jinkins rigged the woodpile around them into a trap. You took the trap apart and got them out.
- **The clinic:** at the [Hand of Spring](compendium.md#hand-of-spring), a man-sized ghostly gecko fought giant centipedes. You killed the centipedes and spared the gecko when [Doctor Dami](compendium.md#doctor-dami) shouted that it was the clinic's guardian spirit.
  - Dami gave you 2 lesser antidotes and a lesser darkvision elixir.
  - He opened his shrine to [Qi Zhong](compendium.md#qi-zhong) for a coin blessing.
  - He gave you a dose of [dreamtime tea](compendium.md#dreamtime-tea) to drug the buso.
- **The Silvermist hunters:** you didn't follow up on Old Matsuki's request for a hunter to track Ugly Cute.

### Blessing the coins

- You blessed the three copper coins at three shrines:
  - [Pharasma](compendium.md#pharasma) at the [Lady of Souls](compendium.md#lady-of-souls). Aseren led this one.
  - [Abadar](compendium.md#abadar) at his shrine.
  - [Qi Zhong](compendium.md#qi-zhong) at the Hand of Spring.

### Dawnstep Bridge

- You watched [Dawnstep Bridge](compendium.md#dawnstep-bridge) from a safe distance first. A big drinker lounged on a throne of stolen furniture while jinkins capered around him.
- This was [Gurglegut](compendium.md#gurglegut), the buso.
- You tried to drug him with the [dreamtime tea](compendium.md#dreamtime-tea). It failed. You fought him head-on and killed him, and the jinkins fled.
- His pockets held a silver scepter set with stones, a full-pack cantrip deck, and three old copper coins that had gone dark.

### The Lantern burns again

- You placed the three blessed coins in the [Eternal Lantern](compendium.md#eternal-lantern)'s cage, and it lit.
- The mist, the crimson moon, and the blood rain ended across town. The animals that had learned to speak became ordinary animals again.
- The gods whose coins you used left a [Lantern boon](compendium.md#lantern-boon) on each of you.
- You reached level 2.

## Chapter 2: Reclaiming Willowshore

> *The Lantern burns again and the fog has lifted. In the clear light the town counts its wounded and its dead, while monsters still hold downtown. The governor's manor stood on its corner yesterday, and this morning its lot is empty.*

### The morning after the Lantern

- A runner brought supplies from the elders for the push downtown: 10 minor healing potions and 8 potency crystals.
- The elders asked the same three things: free the trapped townsfolk, defeat the last two ringleaders ([Gray Butcher](compendium.md#gray-butcher) and [Mo Douqiu](compendium.md#mo-douqiu)), and restore order.
- The [Governor's Manor](compendium.md#governors-manor) was gone. Only an empty lot remained, with two defaced stone lions that once flanked its gate. Nobody in town could say how it happened.

### The stables and the stage

- The downtown stables held a dozen dead horses. A swarm of cockroaches nested in the bodies and attacked. You destroyed it.
- Three jinkins in stolen costumes put on an insulting play about Willowshore's "cowards" from the roof of the [public stage](compendium.md#public-stage). They fled town once one of them got hurt.

### The bathhouse

- In the [Happy Kappa](compendium.md#happy-kappa) bathhouse, the owner [De-ge Hua](compendium.md#de-ge-hua) had squeezed himself into a locker and gotten stuck. You got him out and took him to the Hand of Spring clinic.
- He told you five [kappas](compendium.md#kappa) were relaxing in his baths.
- The kappas invited you to bathe. They called the invasion "unfortunate" and said they weren't the problem. The jinkins killed the stable horses, and the kappas only ate the leftovers.
- They said [Mo Douqiu](compendium.md#mo-douqiu) invited them to help take the town.
- Rai talked them into leaving. They tipped you a pearl, as if you worked at the bathhouse, and headed back to [Gourd Lake](compendium.md#gourd-lake).

### Mudwall House and the Sanmi family

- Six drunk jinkins were wrecking [Mudwall House](compendium.md#mudwall-house). They took you for oni and started showing off. You fought them and won.
- The jinkins had forced two young people to serve them: [Yuli Sanmi](compendium.md#yuli-sanmi) and [Hu Lelong](compendium.md#hu-lelong).
- Lelong asked to keep one of the stolen necklaces. He claimed his uncle, Captain [Zheng Peng](compendium.md#zheng-peng), or Granny Hu would find its owner. You pressed him, and he admitted the truth: he wanted money to run away with Yuli.
- Among the stolen jewelry, Nezu found a necklace that belongs to his own family. The jinkins had [cursed it](compendium.md#cursed-family-necklace). Nezu also took the other 24 pieces, worth about 75 gp, which belong to people around town.
- [Nadoya Sanmi](compendium.md#nadoya-sanmi) and her family were shut in the iron-doored storehouse out back. She asked whether the jinkins were gone, and whether Yuli and "the other young man" were safe. She gave you a crying angel pendant in thanks.
- In the Sanmi house you found letters hidden under a dresser drawer. They reveal a secret romance between Yuli and Lelong. Kurizuki knew the traditional Sanmi family wouldn't approve. Kurizuki kept the letters.

### The barracks

- Captain [Zheng Peng](compendium.md#zheng-peng) and ten guards sat locked in the barracks cells, too weak to fight. You found the spare keys under a loose brick and freed them.
- Zheng told you a cloaked man led the jinkins who attacked the barracks. He doesn't know where they went.
- Zheng gave you the armory key and told you to take what you needed. You took cold iron and silver weapons, frost vials, and a +1 spear.
- In the yard, a trail of bloody prints led from the stables toward the bathhouse.
- You freed four townsfolk from a bamboo cage and walked them to safety. They had been told to "repent" or be sent to the teahouse, and they named the [Cerulean Teahouse](compendium.md#cerulean-teahouse).
- You found the [Shelyn shrine](compendium.md#shelyn-shrine) boarded up, its statue unharmed. Nezu wondered who knew to board it up ahead of time.

### The cookware parade

- You heard it before you saw it: hundreds of pots, pans, and cleavers clattering along to off-key singing. [Gray Butcher](compendium.md#gray-butcher), a living butcher's apron, led two swarms of [animated cookware](compendium.md#animated-cookware).
- Nezu recognized him as an [ittan-momen](compendium.md#ittan-momen).
- The swarms attacked first. Gray Butcher wrapped Aseren up and squeezed. Kurizuki landed a critical hit, and you destroyed him.
- His apron pockets held a +1 silver dagger in a silk-wrapped sheath, 2 potency crystals, 2 mesmerizing opals, and 2 oils of potency.
- You rested for the night.

### The Cerulean Teahouse

- You entered the [Cerulean Teahouse](compendium.md#cerulean-teahouse). The ground floor is wrecked, with gouged walls, broken pots, and an overturned table.
- A [giant toad](compendium.md#giant-toad) attacked. It mauled Nezu and grabbed Aseren in its jaws before you killed it.
- **This is where you stopped.** See [Where we are now](now.md).
