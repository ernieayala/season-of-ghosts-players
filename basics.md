The town and its customs, as your characters knew them **before** the festival. Some of this changed on the first morning. The Eternal Lantern went out, for one. For what happened since, see the [History](history.md).

## Your Story Begins

You live in **Willowshore**, a small lumber town (pop. 225) in rural Shenmen. You were chosen as one of this year's "abductees" for the **Reenactment Festival**, which tricks evil spirits during the "season of ghosts" in early summer.

You spent the night in a forest clearing. At dawn, Choe Chung-hu (the miller) was due to fetch you with breakfast. He never showed up.

The adventure began on the **first day of summer, 7108**.

---

## The Campaign

### Campaign Style
- **Horror themes:** spirits, undead, and supernatural threats.
- **Hometown heroes:** you're defending YOUR community. You live here.
- **Four adventures** across one full year (Summer → Fall → Winter → Spring).
- **Lots of downtime** between major events, for crafting, relationships, and town activities.

### Your Motivation
You're protecting your home, your neighbors, and your loved ones. Community and compassion drive this story, more than gold or glory.

## Gameplay Quick Reference

### Combat Focus
- Expect **incorporeal and spirit enemies.** Weapons that hurt spirits help.
- **Forest terrain** is common.
- **Horror creatures and undead.**

### Social & Downtime
- **Build relationships** with the townsfolk.
- **Faction reputation** matters (Southbank vs. Northridge). See [Reputation](compendium.md#reputation).
- **Crafting** becomes important after the first adventure.
- **Survival skills** matter over a full year in a rural town.

### Most Useful Skills
**Always useful:** Athletics, Diplomacy, Medicine, Nature, Religion, Society, Stealth, Survival

**Important later:** Arcana (second half), Crafting (after Adventure 1), Occultism (second half)

**MVP skill:** **Willowshore Lore**, the most important Lore skill in the campaign.

**Other good Lores:** Academia, Art, Engineering, Farming, Hunting, Sangpotshi, Tea

## Willowshore at a Glance

### The Two Factions
Most townsfolk lean toward one of two groups. Many stay neutral.

**[Southbank](compendium.md#southbank) Traditionalists** (south of the Ceiba River)
- Value tradition, self-sufficiency, and farming.
- Keep the old ways alive.
- Led by "Old" Matsuki Shou.

**[Northridge](compendium.md#northridge) Pragmatists** (north of the Ceiba River)
- Value growth, trade, and education.
- Look toward the future.
- Mixed leadership. Granny Hu speaks for them now.

Both sides put Willowshore's survival first.

## Recent History

**7106** (2 years ago)
- The Lung Wa empire collapsed.
- Terrible winter storms hit, the worst in memory.
- The lumber lords fled Willowshore.

**7107** (last year)
- A milder winter, to the town's relief.
- The town adjusted to life without the empire.

**7108** (now)
- Townsfolk are nervous about another harsh winter.
- Rumors say jorogumo (spider creatures) rule Shenmen.
- Rumors say Sze's ruler has returned as a ghost.
- The traditional "season of ghosts" is starting.

## The Reenactment Festival

**Purpose:** trick evil spirits into thinking Willowshore has already been haunted, so they leave the town alone.

**Tradition:**
1. A big feast on the last day of spring.
2. People in paper masks fake-kidnap the chosen "abductees."
3. The town stages a dramatic panic and mourning.
4. The abductees spend the night in the forest.
5. At dawn, the festival leader brings breakfast as the "ransom."
6. The abductees return, and the town celebrates.

## Important Traditions & Beliefs

### The Season of Ghosts
The first few weeks of summer. Spirits, hauntings, and undead are more active in the woods, and people say ghosts lure travelers to their deaths. The festival and the [Eight Practices](compendium.md#eight-practices) protect the town.

### The Eternal Lantern
An artifact at Dawnstep Bridge. Master Zhi Hui, the monastery's founder, blessed it. Townsfolk believed it would burn forever, and it stands for the town's safety. See the [Eternal Lantern](compendium.md#eternal-lantern) entry for what happened to it.

### The Tan Sugi Monastery
An abandoned monastery in the hills to the west. Founded in 7020, abandoned in 7060. In 7062 came the **Night of Broken Blades**: woodcutters came back as undead and killed dozens. Locals call the place cursed. Few go there.

### Sangpotshi Philosophy
Many Willowshorers follow this path toward enlightenment. The monastery was built for it.

## Your Character

### Seasonal Boons
If you chose a Season of Ghosts background, you have a **Seasonal Boon**. It's a strong ability you can use **once per adventure** (once per season). Save it for when it counts.

### Languages
- **Common** in this campaign is **Tien**, the language of Tian Xia.
- Other useful languages: Abyssal, Aklo, Necril, Sylvan.

## Quick Survival Tips

1. **Know your neighbors.** These NPCs are your friends and family.
2. **Track your reputation.** Your actions change how Southbank and Northridge see you.
3. **Use your seasonal boon.** You get it once per adventure.
4. **Downtime matters.** Use it to craft, build relationships, and prepare.
5. **Spirits are real.** Incorporeal foes are common, so prepare for them.
6. **Winter is coming.** The town still remembers the harsh winter of 7106.
7. **Work together.** The town survives as a community.
