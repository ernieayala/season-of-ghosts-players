## The premise

You five grew up in [Willowshore](compendium.md#willowshore), a small lumber town in rural Shenmen, split by a river into [Northridge](compendium.md#northridge) and [Southbank](compendium.md#southbank). Every year on the last day of spring, the town holds the [Reenactment Festival](compendium.md#reenactment-festival). Townsfolk fake-kidnap a few "abductees" to trick evil spirits into leaving the town alone, and this year they chose you.

The campaign runs one full year, one adventure per season. You're in the first: **summer**.

## Chapter 1: To Light the Night

> *You walked home from the festival woods and found monsters in the streets.*

The miller never came to fetch you, and centipedes that spoke hunted you on the trail. Fog swallowed the town by day, and a blood-red moon hung over it by night. The town's stone guardian, [Ugly Cute](compendium.md#ugly-cute), was gone from its gate. The [Eternal Lantern](compendium.md#eternal-lantern), which had protected Willowshore for generations, had gone dark.

You found the two elders, [Old Matsuki](compendium.md#matsuki-shou-old-matsuki) of Southbank and [Granny Hu](compendium.md#hu-ban-niang-granny-hu) of Northridge, and ran errands for both without taking a side. With three coins blessed at three shrines and the drunken [Gurglegut](compendium.md#gurglegut) dead on Dawnstep Bridge, you relit the Lantern. The fog lifted that day, and the blood moon and the blood rain ended with it.

## Chapter 2: Reclaiming Willowshore

> *The Lantern burned again, but monsters still held the heart of town.*

Downtown was still in monster hands, and the governor's [manor had vanished](compendium.md#governors-manor) overnight. Street by street, you took it back. You freed the guard captain and his men from their own cells and routed a houseful of drunk jinkins, stumbling onto a hidden romance along the way. You talked five kappas out of a bathhouse. Then you destroyed [Gray Butcher](compendium.md#gray-butcher) and his parade of living cookware.

One leader remains: [Mo Douqiu](compendium.md#mo-douqiu). You've fought your way into his base, the [Cerulean Teahouse](compendium.md#cerulean-teahouse).
