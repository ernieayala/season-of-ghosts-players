## The premise

[Willowshore](compendium.md#willowshore) is a small lumber town in rural Shenmen, split by a river into [Northridge](compendium.md#northridge) and [Southbank](compendium.md#southbank). Every year on the last day of spring, the town holds the [Reenactment Festival](compendium.md#reenactment-festival). Townsfolk fake-kidnap a few "abductees" to trick evil spirits into leaving the town alone. This year they chose five: Aseren, Kurizuki, Nezu, Rai, and Yamano. Chapters 1 and 2 are their story.

The campaign runs one full year, one adventure per season. You're in the first: **summer**.

## Chapter 1: To Light the Night

> *You walked home from the festival woods and found monsters in the streets.*

The miller never came to fetch you, and centipedes that spoke hunted you on the trail. Fog swallowed the town by day, and a blood-red moon hung over it by night. The town's stone guardian, [Ugly Cute](compendium.md#ugly-cute), was gone from its gate. The [Eternal Lantern](compendium.md#eternal-lantern), which had protected Willowshore for generations, had gone dark.

You found the two elders, [Old Matsuki](compendium.md#matsuki-shou-old-matsuki) of Southbank and [Granny Hu](compendium.md#hu-ban-niang-granny-hu) of Northridge, and ran errands for both without taking a side. With three coins blessed at three shrines and the drunken [Gurglegut](compendium.md#gurglegut) dead on Dawnstep Bridge, you relit the Lantern. The fog lifted that day, and the blood moon and the blood rain ended with it.

## Chapter 2: Reclaiming Willowshore

> *The Lantern burned again, but monsters still held the heart of town.*

Downtown was still in monster hands, and the governor's [manor had vanished](compendium.md#governors-manor) overnight. Street by street, you took it back. You freed the guard captain and his men from their own cells and routed a houseful of drunk jinkins, stumbling onto a hidden romance along the way. You talked five kappas out of a bathhouse. Then you destroyed [Gray Butcher](compendium.md#gray-butcher) and his parade of living cookware.

The last leader, [Mo Douqiu](compendium.md#mo-douqiu), held the [Cerulean Teahouse](compendium.md#cerulean-teahouse). You fought through it, freed the twelve townsfolk he kept locked in the pantry, and killed him and his creatures. On the counter you found the will of the teahouse's last owner, [Qing Mai-Lai](compendium.md#qing-mai-lai). Downtown was free.

## Chapter 3

> *The monsters were gone, and a stranger's cart came rattling down the empty street.*

A traveling merchant, [Asahina Shinzo](compendium.md#asahina-shinzo), set up a stall downtown, and you traded with him. Nezu handed the stolen jewelry to [Granny Hu](compendium.md#hu-ban-niang-granny-hu) to return to its owners, and she sent you to [Karahai](compendium.md#karahai) on horseback. You never arrived. A few hours short of the coast a [strange fog](compendium.md#strange-fog) rolled in, and when it lifted you stood exactly where you'd started. It happened every time you tried.

Back in town, both elders thanked you and asked you to look into three mysteries: the missing governor, the fog, and the missing [Ugly Cute](compendium.md#ugly-cute).

Kurizuki, Rai, and Yamano took up the fog without Nezu. They brought wine to the spirit of the [Great Willow](compendium.md#great-willow), who sent them south to save a friend's [willow grove](compendium.md#willow-grove) from a mushroom creature. The creature killed all three. Nezu is the last of the five abductees still on the elders' case, and new defenders now stand with him.
