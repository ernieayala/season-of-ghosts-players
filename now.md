- [Granny Hu](compendium.md#hu-ban-niang-granny-hu) and [Old Matsuki](compendium.md#matsuki-shou-old-matsuki) met you together at the [Lady of Souls](compendium.md#lady-of-souls). They thanked you and asked you to look into three mysteries.

## The three mysteries

The elders want all three looked into, in any order. They think the governor is the most pressing, but you decide where to start. You can switch between them.

### The missing governor

**What you know**

- Governor [Heh Shan-Bao](compendium.md#heh-shan-bao) is gone, and so is his [manor](compendium.md#governors-manor).
- The morning after you relit the Lantern, the manor's lot downtown stood empty. Two defaced stone lions that once guarded its gate are all that's left.
- Nobody in town can say how it happened.

**You could**

- Walk the empty lot yourselves and search it.
- Ask the elders what they know about the governor and anyone who might have wanted him gone.
- Talk to Captain [Zheng Peng](compendium.md#zheng-peng), who commands the town guard.
- Ask the townsfolk who live and work downtown what they remember from that night.

### The fog

**What you know**

- A few hours short of Karahai, past [Gourd Lake](compendium.md#gourd-lake), a fog rolled in fast and thick. When it lifted, you stood where you started. It happened every time you pushed on.
- It isn't the [mirage mist](compendium.md#mirage-mist). That ended when you relit the Lantern.
- The elders want to know how far it reaches. Does it only block the road to the coast, or does it ring the whole valley?
- Nothing any of you knew explained it.

**You could**

- Try another road or direction and see where the fog meets you there.
- Ask the town's priests and scholars about curses: [Elizeth Candora](compendium.md#elizeth-candora) at the Lady of Souls, [Ba-Ming Ouh](compendium.md#ba-ming-ouh) at the Nine Ear Shrine, or the wizard [Igawa Jubei](compendium.md#igawa-jubei) at Mother's Coil.
- Visit the [Great Willow](compendium.md#great-willow), the sacred tree on the mountain above town.
- Ask the elders who might know the land well enough to judge where the fog runs.

### Ugly Cute

**What you know**

- [Ugly Cute](compendium.md#ugly-cute), the horse-sized stone spider, guarded the [Spider Gate](compendium.md#spider-gate) for generations.
- On the first morning its post stood empty. Its lantern lay unlit in the grass, and deep tracks led down the road and into the river. You didn't follow them then.
- Old Matsuki wanted the [Silvermist hunters](compendium.md#silvermist-hunters) to help track it. You haven't asked them yet.

**You could**

- Go back to the Spider Gate and look at the tracks and the riverbank again.
- Find a Silvermist hunter and ask for help tracking a stone spider.
- Ask around town whether anyone has seen Ugly Cute since the festival.

## Other things you could do

These aren't the elders' request. They're threads your characters know about, and you can pick them up between mysteries.

### Mai-Lai's will

- You hold "The Last Will and Testament of [Qing Mai-Lai](compendium.md#qing-mai-lai)." You took it from the counter of the [Cerulean Teahouse](compendium.md#cerulean-teahouse). Mai-Lai was the teahouse's last owner.
- You've read it. Mai-Lai left no living heirs, so the teahouse goes to the first person who recovers the "two pearls I've thrown into Fumeiyoshi's eyes."
- Locals call two ponds north of town the "Eyes of Fumeiyoshi."
- You could go after the pearls and claim the teahouse, or take the will to [Luo Xi Yang](compendium.md#luo-xi-yang), the town lawyer, first.

### The Sanmi family

- [Nadoya Sanmi](compendium.md#nadoya-sanmi) and her family are still in the iron-doored storehouse behind [Mudwall House](compendium.md#mudwall-house), where the jinkins shut them in.
- You could check on them and see them somewhere safe.
- [Yuli Sanmi](compendium.md#yuli-sanmi) and [Hu Lelong](compendium.md#hu-lelong) want to run away together. Kurizuki still has their [love letters](compendium.md#love-letters).

### Shopping with Shinzo

- [Asahina Shinzo](compendium.md#asahina-shinzo) sells everyday goods from his cart and buys what you don't need.
- If you want something he doesn't carry, you could ask him to bring it on his next visit.

### Loose ends

- **[Choe Chung-hu](compendium.md#choe-chung-hu)**, the miller who never came with breakfast, is still missing.
- **[Hai-er Ha](compendium.md#hai-er-ha):** you left her at the Eastern Watchtower. Her daughter was with Granny Hu, and her husband was at the Matsuki estate. You could find out whether she made it home.
- **The [Shelyn shrine](compendium.md#shelyn-shrine):** someone boarded it up before the monsters came. Nobody has said who.

## Your standing in town

Each half of town, [Northridge](compendium.md#northridge) and [Southbank](compendium.md#southbank), tracks how you treat its people. This is your [Reputation](compendium.md#reputation). Helping people raises it, and hurting them or what they care about lowers it.

Both halves of town count you as **Liked**. You haven't sided with either elder.

| Faction | Standing | Townsfolk start | Social checks | Earn Income |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Northridge](compendium.md#northridge) | **Liked** | Friendly | 2 easier | +1 |
| [Southbank](compendium.md#southbank) | **Liked** | Friendly | 2 easier | +1 |

What "Liked" gets you with that faction's people:

- **Friendly to start.** Townsfolk greet you as friends. They're willing to help, within reason.
- **Social checks are 2 easier.** When you try to persuade, trick, or pressure someone, with skills such as Diplomacy, Deception, or Intimidation, the number you need to roll is 2 lower.
- **+1 to Earn Income.** Earn Income is a downtime activity. You spend days working at a job with a skill, such as Crafting, Performance, or a Lore, and roll once to see how well it pays each day. You get +1 to that roll when you work for people or businesses in either half of town.

The [Reputation](compendium.md#reputation) entry lists the full ladder, from Hunted to Revered.
